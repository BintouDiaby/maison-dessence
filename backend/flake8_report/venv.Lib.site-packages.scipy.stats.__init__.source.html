4 ==========================================
5 Statistical functions (:mod:`scipy.stats`)
6 ==========================================
7  
8 .. currentmodule:: scipy.stats
9  
10 This module contains a large number of probability distributions,
11 summary and frequency statistics, correlation functions and statistical
12 tests, masked statistics, kernel density estimation, quasi-Monte Carlo
13 functionality, and more.
14  
15 Statistics is a very large area, and there are topics that are out of scope
16 for SciPy and are covered by other packages. Some of the most important ones
17 are:
18  
19 - `statsmodels <https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/index.html>`__:
20 regression, linear models, time series analysis, extensions to topics
21 also covered by ``scipy.stats``.
22 - `Pandas <https://pandas.pydata.org/>`__: tabular data, time series
23 functionality, interfaces to other statistical languages.
24 - `PyMC <https://docs.pymc.io/>`__: Bayesian statistical
25 modeling, probabilistic machine learning.
26 - `scikit-learn <https://scikit-learn.org/>`__: classification, regression,
27 model selection.
28 - `Seaborn <https://seaborn.pydata.org/>`__: statistical data visualization.
29 - `rpy2 <https://rpy2.github.io/>`__: Python to R bridge.
30

297 Hypothesis Tests and related functions
298 ======================================
299 SciPy has many functions for performing hypothesis tests that return a
300 test statistic and a p-value, and several of them return confidence intervals
301 and/or other related information.
302  
303 The headings below are based on common uses of the functions within, but due to
304 the wide variety of statistical procedures, any attempt at coarse-grained
305 categorization will be imperfect. Also, note that tests within the same heading
306 are not interchangeable in general (e.g. many have different distributional
307 assumptions).
308  
309 One Sample Tests / Paired Sample Tests
310 --------------------------------------
311 One sample tests are typically used to assess whether a single sample was
312 drawn from a specified distribution or a distribution with specified properties
313 (e.g. zero mean).
314  
315 .. autosummary::
316 :toctree: generated/
317  
318 ttest_1samp
319 binomtest
320 quantile_test
321 skewtest
322 kurtosistest
323 normaltest
324 jarque_bera
325 shapiro
326 anderson
327 cramervonmises
328 ks_1samp
329 goodness_of_fit
330 chisquare
331 power_divergence
332  
333 Paired sample tests are often used to assess whether two samples were drawn
334 from the same distribution; they differ from the independent sample tests below
335 in that each observation in one sample is treated as paired with a
336 closely-related observation in the other sample (e.g. when environmental
337 factors are controlled between observations within a pair but not among pairs).
338 They can also be interpreted or used as one-sample tests (e.g. tests on the
339 mean or median of *differences* between paired observations).
340

342 :toctree: generated/
